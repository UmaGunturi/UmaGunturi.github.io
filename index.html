I work on natural language processing and computational social science, with a growing focus on cultural analytics. I study online communities and the narratives people share in them: how people tell stories about their lived experiences, and how those stories reflect, reinforce, or push back against dominant narratives and social power. I am also interested in how language technologies interpret these culturally rich narratives. My recent work on how people share counter-stories across Reddit communities will appear at EMNLP 2026.

I am an affiliated researcher in the Culture, Language, and Systems (CLS) Lab at CU Boulder, working with Dr. Maria Antoniak, and a Machine Learning Engineer at IBM, where I build expressive neural voices for IBM's Text to Speech service. I earned my M.S. in Computer Science from Virginia Tech, advised by Dr. Eugenia H. Rho in the SAIL Lab, where I wrote my thesis on racial microaggressions on social media.

On the PhD market I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027 in NLP, computational social science, and cultural analytics. Happy to chat. Feel free to email me!
